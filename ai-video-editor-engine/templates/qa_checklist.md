# QA checklist (tick with evidence; save evidence in report/qa/)

- [ ] Runtime and aspect ratio confirmed; brand assets licensed; voice and music source confirmed
- [ ] Hook readable in under 1 s; product or promise visible by 3 s (30 s film) or 8 s (60 s film)
- [ ] Contact sheet every 0.5 s reviewed; every transition checked at 3–4 points
- [ ] No pops, stepped motion blur, clipped or overlapping text, safe-area violations
- [ ] Logo untouched and pixel-exact at rest; nothing drawn over it
- [ ] Text readable at 720p on a phone; 7 words or fewer per line; captions burned in for social
- [ ] Every visible move has a sound; whooshes only for movement; no dings, bells or hums
- [ ] Loudness measured: integrated ___ LUFS (target ___), true peak ___ dBTP (<= -1.0), LRA ___
- [ ] Music at least 12 dB under every spoken word (or 8–10 dB under the SFX stem when no VO)
- [ ] Each hit: visual contact frame and audio onset within 1 frame
- [ ] 9:16 version is a real re-layout (not a crop); type re-wrapped; viewed at phone size
- [ ] Claims true and approved; personal details removed from screenshots; spec-ad line present if needed
- [ ] Honest report written: what was checked (stills, sheets, numbers) and what was not (real-time playback, listening)
- [ ] Score 0–5: Hook __ Clarity __ Hierarchy __ Motion __ Sound __ Brand __ CTA __  (fix anything below 4)
